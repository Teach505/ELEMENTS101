Review naming patterns (-gen, -ine, -on, -ium), Latin symbols, discoverers, and textbook properties.

Suffix "-gen" (H, N, O) Nonmetals that exist as gases at room temperature.
Suffix "-ine" (F, Cl) Belong to the halogens; highly reactive nonmetals.
Suffix "-on" (Ne, Ar) Noble gases, known for being inert or nonreactive.
Suffix "-ium" (Na, Mg, Ca...) Metals: typically hard, shiny, and good conductors of heat & electricity.
